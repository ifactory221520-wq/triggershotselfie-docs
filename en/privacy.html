Privacy Policy

Trigger Shot Selfie / Effective: November 21, 2026

This app does not send camera video, your registered poses, or the photos and videos you take to the developer. All pose recognition happens on your iPhone. The developer has no way of knowing what you photograph.

1. Information the app uses

  • Front camera video: used to show the preview, recognize your registered trigger poses, and take photos and videos. The video itself is processed on the spot and discarded.
  • Microphone audio: used only while recording to add sound to videos. Audio is kept only inside the videos you record.
  • Registered pose data: body joint positions, facial expression values, a small preview image, and a body outline. Used to recognize your poses and to show the on-screen guide.
  • App settings, and the date your free trial started and the date you purchased.

The app never collects your name, phone number, email address, contacts, location, or payment details.

2. Where it is stored

Registered pose data and settings are stored only on your iPhone (in the app's own storage). The app has no developer servers.

3. Photos library

The app uses add-only access to your Photos library to save the photos and videos you take. The app cannot see any other photos in your library.

4. When the app communicates

The app communicates only with Apple (the App Store) when you purchase or restore a purchase. Payment information is handled by Apple and is never passed to the developer.

5. Ads and tracking

The app shows no ads. It uses no identifiers for tracking users or devices, includes no analytics services (including crash reporting), and has no sign-in.

6. Deletion

  • When you re-register a pose, the previous pose data is overwritten and removed.
  • Deleting the app removes all registered poses and settings from your iPhone. No copy remains with the developer (nothing was ever sent).
  • Photos and videos you took are in the Photos app; delete them there.

7. Children

The app is not directed at any particular age group and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its date.
