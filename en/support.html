Trigger Shot Selfie

About this app

Set your iPhone down and strike a registered trigger pose (full-body posture and facial expression), and the front camera takes photos or videos automatically. You can register two poses: one for photos (blue) and one for videos (red).

Pricing

All features are free for 30 days after installation. To keep using the app after that, a one-time purchase is required. There is no subscription. See the App Store for the price.

Poses and settings you made during the trial stay as they are after purchase.

FAQ

Shooting rarely starts

  • Stand where your full body and face are visible, as when you registered.
  • Set “Detection strictness” in Settings to “Loose”.
  • Poses are easier to recognize in a bright place.

Shooting starts on its own

  • Set “Detection strictness” in Settings to “Strict”.
  • Re-register a pose with clearly different arm positions or hand shapes from how you normally stand.

The photo and video poses get mixed up

If the two poses are similar, you will see “⚠ Your two poses look similar” when registering. Re-register with different arm positions or hand shapes.

I can't turn off the shutter sound

In some regions, including Japan, iPhone rules do not allow the photo shutter sound to be turned off. Turning off “Shutter and countdown sounds” in Settings silences the countdown and other sounds.

Photos look flipped

The preview is shown like a mirror, while saved photos and videos use the true orientation (text in the background reads correctly), the same as the iPhone Camera app.

The camera stops after a while

If nothing is captured for a while, the camera stops to save battery and prevent heat. Tap the screen to resume. You can change the time in Settings → “Auto-stop standby”.

I got a new iPhone. Do I need to buy again?

With the same Apple Account, go to Settings → “Purchase” → “Restore Purchase”.

Is camera video sent to the developer?

No. See the Privacy Policy for details.
